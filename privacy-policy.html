Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 2026

QuickBuy HQ (“we”, “us”, or “our”) respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal information. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store and protect your data when you use our website and services.

1. Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

  • Personal details — name, email address, phone number, delivery address
  • Account information — login credentials and profile preferences
  • Order information — products purchased, payment status, delivery details
  • Device & usage data — IP address, browser type, pages visited, and interactions with our site
  • Communication data — messages you send us via contact forms, email or WhatsApp

2. How We Use Your Information

We use your information to:

  • Process and deliver your orders
  • Communicate with you about orders, delivery and support
  • Create and manage your account
  • Improve our website, products and customer experience
  • Send important updates and (with your consent) promotional offers
  • Prevent fraud and ensure the security of our platform
  • Comply with legal obligations

3. How We Share Your Information

We do not sell your personal data. We may share limited information with:

  • Delivery partners — to fulfil and deliver your orders
  • Payment processors — to securely process payments
  • Service providers — who help us operate the website (hosting, analytics, customer support tools)
  • Legal authorities — when required by law or to protect our rights

All third parties are required to handle your data responsibly and only for the purpose of providing services to us.

4. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies and similar technologies to improve site performance, remember your preferences, and understand how visitors use our website. You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Note that some features may not work properly if cookies are disabled.

5. Data Security

We take appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect your personal information against unauthorised access, loss, misuse or alteration. While no system is 100% secure, we continuously work to safeguard your data.

6. Data Retention

We keep your personal information only for as long as necessary to fulfil the purposes outlined in this policy, including order history, legal requirements, and legitimate business needs. When data is no longer needed, we securely delete or anonymise it.

7. Your Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction of inaccurate information
  • Request deletion of your data (subject to legal exceptions)
  • Object to or restrict certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing is based on consent

To exercise any of these rights, please contact us using the details below.

8. Children’s Privacy

Our services are not directed at children under 16. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, please contact us so we can take appropriate action.

9. Third-Party Links

Our website may contain links to external websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those third-party sites. We encourage you to read their privacy policies.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. The latest version will always be available on this page with the updated date. Continued use of our services after changes means you accept the revised policy.
